Questions

Ask before you drive out

Still unsure? Ask on Discord or call 541-799-6823.

Do I need my own gear?

“Yes. Every player brings their own marker, mask and BBs. Gear rentals are not available at this time.”

What eye protection qualifies?

Full-seal ANSI Z87.1+ eye protection. Mesh goggles are prohibited. Eye pro stays on until you are inside the designated safe zone. Players under 18 must wear full-face protection.

What is chrono, and what happens if I fail?

Every replica must be chronographed and checked in by staff before entering the field, and is marked once approved. Any replica not chronographed, checked in and marked may not be used. Random re-chrono may occur; replicas over the limit are removed from play. See the FPS limits.

How does the medic rule work?

One designated medic per team, identified by an armband. Revives are performed by attaching a zip tie to the downed player’s gear. Players may only be revived once per life unless event rules state otherwise.

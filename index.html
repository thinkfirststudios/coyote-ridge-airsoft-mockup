SEC 03 · First timers

Your first game day

Five steps, in order. The second one decides whether you can play.

  1. 01

    Sign the waiver before you arriveTwo minutes, covers a year of game days. Waiver

  2. 02

    Bring your own gear — marker, mask and BBs.Rentals are not available at this time.

  3. 03

    Eye protectionFull-seal ANSI Z87.1+ eye protection. Mesh goggles are prohibited. Eye pro stays on until you are inside the designated safe zone. Players under 18 must wear full-face protection.

  4. 04

    Chrono before playEvery replica is checked in and marked by staff.

  5. 05

    Mandatory safety briefingThen games, 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM.

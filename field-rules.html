SEC 01

General safety

  • Full-seal ANSI Z87.1+ eye protection in all active play areas; mesh goggles prohibited.
  • Eye pro stays on until inside the designated safe zone; removing it in an active area means immediate removal.
  • Barrel covers on, magazines out, chambers cleared in the safe zone.
  • No blind firing — you must see your target.
  • No physical contact, aggressive behaviour, drugs or alcohol.
  • Treat every replica as if it is loaded.
SEC 02

Required protective gear

  • Players under 18 must wear full-face protection.
  • Players 18+ must wear full-seal ANSI Z87.1+ eye protection.
  • Lower face protection strongly recommended.
Eye protection, in full

Full-seal ANSI Z87.1+ eye protection. Mesh goggles are prohibited. Eye pro stays on until you are inside the designated safe zone. Players under 18 must wear full-face protection.

SEC 03

FPS / power limits

As the table above. Only true SMG replicas may use controlled full-auto up to 25 RPS within the 380 limit. Qualifying platforms:

  • MP5
  • MP7
  • MP9
  • UMP
  • P90
  • KRISS Vector
  • CZ Scorpion EVO
  • MAC-10
  • Uzi
  • and similar
AR-platform exclusion

AR-platform replicas are NOT SMGs regardless of barrel length, stock, magazine or caliber conversion — semi only.

  • Bio BBs required.
  • Chrono required before play.
  • HPA must be tournament locked.
  • Random chrono and joule creep checks may occur; replicas over the limit are removed from play.
SEC 04

LMG / support weapons

  • Replica support weapons only (M249, PKM, RPK etc).
  • No converted rifles.
  • Box or drum magazines only.

SEC 06

Hits & gameplay

  • Any hit to body or gear counts.
  • Ricochets and gun hits do not.
  • Call “HIT!”, raise your hand or dead rag, head to respawn.
  • Dead men don’t talk — no relaying enemy positions once hit.
  • Cheating: warning, then removal.
SEC 07

Medic rule

  1. One designated medic per team, identified by an armband.
  2. Revives are performed by attaching a zip tie to the downed player’s gear.
  3. Players may only be revived once per life unless event rules state otherwise.
SEC 08

Safe zone

  • No firing or dry firing.
  • Barrel covers on, magazines removed, chambers cleared.
  • Masks may only be removed inside the safe zone.
SEC 09

Banned items

Prohibited

  • Real firearms, live weapons, knives, blades or sharp weapons in gameplay areas.
  • Fireworks, explosives, homemade devices, or anything not approved by staff.
  • Lasers aimed at eyes or used unsafely.
  • Smoke grenades, pyrotechnics, thunder flashes, blank-firing and CO2 sound grenades are prohibited.
  • Any device producing flame, heat, sparks, smoke, combustion or loud explosive sound is prohibited.
  • No smoking due to wildfire risk.

Permitted

  • Spring-powered reusable airsoft grenades ARE permitted.
  • Vaping only in designated staging areas.
SEC 10

Player conduct

  • Respect players, staff and property.
  • No harassment, excessive overshooting or aggressive behaviour.
  • Pack out your trash.
  • Intentionally damaging field structures or vegetation may result in removal.
  • Violations may result in removal without refund.
SEC 11

Staff authority

Referees have final authority on all rulings and may remove unsafe players at any time.

SEC 12

Chronograph & compliance

  • Every replica must be chronographed and checked in by staff before entering the field.
  • Once approved, the replica is marked to show it passed inspection.
  • Any replica not chronographed, checked in and marked may not be used.
  • Staff may conduct random inspections and re-chrono at any time.
SEC 13

Safety briefing

Attendance is mandatory before participating.

SEC 14

Waiver & age

  • All participants must sign a waiver.
  • Minors require a parent or guardian signature.

SEC 15

Emergencies

“BLIND MAN!”

Anyone may call “BLIND MAN!”

  1. Immediately stop shooting.
  2. Repeat the call.
  3. Remain in place and await instructions.

First aid is available and emergency services will be contacted if needed.

SEC 16

Weather

Games may be delayed or cancelled due to unsafe conditions.

SEC 17

Registration & no-shows

  • Register only if you intend to attend.
  • Notify staff if plans change.
  • No-shows without notice may be blacklisted.

“Participation means you agree to follow all field rules and referee decisions, and to play with honesty, safety, and good sportsmanship.”
